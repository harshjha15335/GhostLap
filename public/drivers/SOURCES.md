# Drivers page image sources

Every image under `public/drivers/` must be listed here with where it came from and the
permission you have to use it. Source files go in `scripts/drivers/source/<CODE>/`
(gitignored). `scripts/drivers/process_driver_media.py` turns them into web images.

**Status: no driver images are bundled yet.** Official driver portraits (formula1.com)
and helmet photos (teams, drivers, photographers, team stores) are copyrighted. Add them
only once you have a licence or written permission, for example from the team or driver
press office or from Formula 1's licensing team. Keep the credit line on the page:

> Driver portraits and helmet images © Formula 1 / respective teams. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Formula 1.

Until then each card shows a labelled stand-in (team colour, number, "portrait / helmet
image not supplied"). No helmet is ever drawn or generated.

What each driver needs:
- a transparent cut-out portrait, head and shoulders or full body. The head should be at
  least ~400 px tall in the source; 4K full-body cut-outs are ideal.
- a transparent cut-out of the 2026 race helmet, front or three-quarter view. If only an
  older helmet image is available, use it and say so in Notes.
