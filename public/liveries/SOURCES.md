# Team livery textures

Livery textures adapted from the community '2026 Chassis' livery packs (credits embedded in the sheets: MVPS, OFFERING, DWN00); team and sponsor marks belong to their owners; unofficial fan project.

- `<teamId>.webp` — 1024×512 atlas for `F1Car` (detail="high")
- `<teamId>-sm.webp` — 512×256 atlas (detail="low" replay cars, small screens)

Generated by `scripts/liveries/build_liveries.py` from the 2048×2048 "main" livery sheets
(the source sheets are not committed). The atlas layout is `LIVERY_ATLAS` in
`src/components/three/liveries.ts`; the crop rectangles mapping sheet panels to atlas
regions live in the build script.

Rebuild:

```sh
python3 scripts/liveries/build_liveries.py <dir-with-teamId-0.png-sheets>
```
