# Albert Park Circuit (Melbourne) — 2026 Visual & Branding Reference

Reference notes compiled for GhostLap 2026's hero Melbourne 3D circuit (`public/models/circuits/melbourne/`). All real-world geometry is derived from OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and aligned directly to the 2026 OpenF1 telemetry racing line (`public/data/2026/races/melbourne.json`). No copyrighted photos or map imagery are bundled in the app.

## 1. 2026 Event Identity & Layout
- **Official 2026 Event Name**: **Formula 1 Qatar Airways Australian Grand Prix 2026** (6–8 March 2026, Round 1, 30th anniversary in Melbourne) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Australian_Grand_Prix), [Forbes Australia](https://www.forbes.com.au/life/f1/moet-chandon-2027-australian-f1-grand-prix/) (note: Moët & Chandon took over title sponsorship for 2027; Louis Vuitton held 2025; Qatar Airways held 2026).
- **Circuit Configuration (2021+ Layout)**: 5.278 km official length, 14 corners, clockwise around Albert Park Lake — [Formula 1 2026 Circuit Guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/circuit-guide-2026-australian-grand-prix-albert-park.19zPlhKhMbTaVNFIPKAAMa), [RacingCircuits.info](https://www.racingcircuits.info/australasia/australia/albert-park,-melbourne.html).
  - Pre-2021 Turn 9–10 chicane **removed** (now a fast sweeping run along Lakeside Drive).
  - Turns 1, 3, 6, and 13 widened; Turn 11–12 reprofiled; pit lane widened.
  - **2026 Special Naming**: Turn 6 dedicated to Laura Mueller (Haas) and Hannah Schmitz (Red Bull) for International Women's Day 2026 — [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/haas-best-off-track-moments-10-years-on-from-their-2016-f1-debut.1hHlGHmncTdVRX4i1EekMe).
