# Shanghai International Circuit — 2026 Chinese Grand Prix Visual & Branding Reference

Reference notes compiled for GhostLap 2026's Shanghai International Circuit 3D environment (`public/models/circuits/shanghai/`). All real-world surroundings are derived from OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and aligned to the 2026 OpenF1 telemetry racing line (`public/data/2026/races/shanghai.json`). No copyrighted photos or map imagery are bundled in the app.

## 1. 2026 Event Identity & Circuit Overview
- **Official 2026 Event Name**: **Formula 1 Heineken Chinese Grand Prix 2026** (13–15 March 2026, Round 2, first Sprint weekend of 2026) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Chinese_Grand_Prix), [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/china).
- **Title Sponsor**: **Heineken** — [Formula 1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026/china), [DAO Insights](https://daoinsights.com/works/heineken-shanghai-f1/).
- **Circuit Configuration**: 5.451 km official length, 16 turns, clockwise, designed by Hermann Tilke in Jiading District (NW Shanghai) inspired by the Chinese character `上` (*shang*) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_International_Circuit), [Formula 1 Track Guide 2026](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/circuit-guide-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-shanghai-international.1b0f0ghbsPMRsHJoHTt6eB).
